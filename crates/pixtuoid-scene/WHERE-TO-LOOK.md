# pixtuoid-scene — where to look

Answers for the questions indexed in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)'s
"Where to look". An entry earns its place only when landing on the
obvious symbol does NOT answer the question — it names a seam, a
split, a chokepoint, or a decision that looks like a bug. Everything
else is the code's job to prove.

- "Where does a furniture's footprint / visual size / approach side / dwell come from?" → **`layout::decor`** — ONE geometry table (`FurnitureDef`); the role enums carry NO dimensions. Load-bearing distinctions: `footprint` ≠ `visual` (may overhang); `occupies_pos` (render) ≠ `exclusive` (capacity); placement overlap (bug) ≠ occlusion overlap (correct).
- "Why doesn't a bigger buffer just render the office sharper?" → `render_scale.rs` is THE layout-space ↔ buffer-space seam; `floor_capacity_is_invariant_under_render_scale` pins it, `without_the_seam_a_bigger_buffer_builds_a_bigger_office` is the negative control.
- "Which side does an agent approach furniture from?" → `layout::approach_point` is **A\*'s goal**, filtered by `FurnitureDef.approach` (rotated by facing) AND `ReachSet`. Seat kinds add a post-A\* SETTLE onto `seated_foot_cell` — don't collapse it into a single `pos` target. Every desk-touching leg shares `pose::desk_leg_endpoint` (a raw blocked goal walks THROUGH the desk). Seating a desk EAST/WEST touches exactly three places: `desk_walk_anchor_facing`, `Seat::view`'s fused arm, the placer's facing.
- "How is the office rendered (pixel pass)?" → **`floor::render_floor` is the shared headless frame seam (#423); `floor::FloorSession` the owned painter session over it** — each painter that hand-rolled the bundle drifted once, so `render()` runs the dual eviction itself, and its scene MUST be the FULL live scene (why `render_floor` never evicts). A FOURTH painter starts from `FloorSession`; `observe` is the headless twin; the TUI's `draw_scene` is the ONE deliberate raw `render_to_rgb_buffer` consumer.
- "How do agent name-badge labels work?" → the MODEL is `overlay::build_overlay`; it carries TONE, not color. The wasm painter exports it as JSON and the site lays DOM spans over the canvas — baked AA text would upscale blocky.
- "How do the room dividers render (frosted-glass partitions)?" → render half `pixel_painter/wall.rs`, geometry half `layout::rooms::walls`, bound by shared consts + shared `stitch_vertical_wall` (they diverged once, #559); the painter never re-infers gaps — door jambs draw from `SceneLayout.doorways`.
- "How does the theme system work?" → `theme/mod.rs`; the guard suite is the part grep won't surface: `source_colors_cover_every_registered_source` pins the field set to the source registry; `tests/site_badge_colors.rs` pins the site's copies.
- "How does weather / sky-light work?" → an ATMOSPHERE, not a light level: brightness = `emitter_lum × atmo`, and `sky::emitter(now) -> SkyState` is the ONE physical light source every derivation (disc, floor spill, wall spot) shares — why they can never disagree. `pixel_painter::force_weather` is the single override chokepoint.
- "How does the meeting room come alive?" → seats are ordinary waypoints — idle agents arrive emergently, no coordinator. `chitchat` is venue-keyed (`VenueKey::Room` merges a room into ONE conversation; the lounge couch collapses via `venue_wp_idx`).
- "What is the elastic wander timeline?" → `advance_wander()` in `motion/mod.rs`; two don'ts grep won't warn about: the stale-resume trigger is a frame-cadence sentinel — do NOT raise it to "max dwell" (off-screen gaps replay the backlog); the `WanderPhase::Seated` arm returns `Pose::SeatedIdle` DIRECTLY — don't fall through to the stateless `idle_pose` (its independent timeline drifts: a teleport).
- "How does the gateway mascot (the OpenClaw lobster) work?" → `creatures.rs`; ONE mascot per daemon INSTANCE. Destinations come from the WHOLE walkable floor — the curated per-furniture list WAS the crowding (measured, deleted; `four_gateways_rarely_crowd_now_that_the_whole_floor_is_in_play`), and destinations no longer encode daemon state (cadence + tint do) — don't re-add claim/probe machinery.
- "How does the lofi soundtrack get composed (and how do I add an instrument / a stem)?" → `audio/compose.rs::compose(mood, seed)` → `audio/synth.rs::gen_beds` through the fingerprint-anchored cores. New instrument = the checklist in `.claude/skills/procedural-lofi`; a new STEM spans `LoopStem` / `TRACK_STEMS` / `StemLevels` / `lane_recipe` + `gen_bed` arm / the wasm+site gain-node counts (the silent-lane and Rain-LAST traps are sharp edges). Quality gate: blind-audition a seed batch, ratify the GENERATOR.
